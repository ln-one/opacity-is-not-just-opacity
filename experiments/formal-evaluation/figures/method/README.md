# Method diagram draft

One fixed set of source colors and shapes is shown on light and dark canvases. The conventional panel is arranged left to right as alpha 0, 0.6, and 1; blue arrows point left, illustrating difference contraction from 1 toward 0. The extension panel is arranged as alpha 1, 1.1, and 1.4; orange arrows point right, illustrating difference expansion. Arrows and both effect labels use matching Tol Vibrant blue (#0077BB) and orange (#EE7733). A thin orange outline with no fill marks the extension panel as the research focus. No equation or extra source-color heading is included. Directional arrows convey contraction and expansion; no additional symbolic icons duplicate the rendered examples.

Source colors are Paul Tol Vibrant orange (#EE7733), blue (#0077BB), and magenta (#EE3377), paired with a circle, square, and triangle. Demo canvases are #FFFFFF and #181818. Colors are computed with the experiment's linear-RGB renderer, clipped, encoded to sRGB, and rounded to 8-bit values. Alpha 1 reproduces the exact source colors on both canvases. Alpha 0 produces the canvas color. These are illustrative cases, not a universal perceptual guarantee. Numeric records are in method-demo-data.json.
