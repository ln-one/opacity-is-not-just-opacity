# Repository instructions

- Work directly on the current branch; do not create branches unless requested.
- Edit manuscript sections in `markdown/`; regenerate the English reader and PDF with `python3 paper/build.py`.
- Preserve the author-reviewed argument, numerical results, failures, and citations. Make local revisions rather than rewriting accepted sections.
- Keep figure source colors, markers and meanings consistent. Use Paul Tol's Vibrant for categorical data, YlOrBr for sequential data, and BuRd for diverging data.
- Inspect rendered PDF pages after layout changes. Keep single-column figures near their discussion and place wide figures independently.
- Recorded results and protocol hashes describe the original runs. Do not overwrite provenance merely to match later tooling changes.
- Do not commit, push, publish, or change repository visibility unless requested.
